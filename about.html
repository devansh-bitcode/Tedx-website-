About TEDx NMIMS Indore

Our Mission

TEDx NMIMS Indore is an independently organized TED event that brings together bright minds to give talks that are idea-focused, and on a wide range of subjects, to foster learning, inspiration and wonder – and provoke conversations that matter.

We believe in the power of ideas to change attitudes, lives and, ultimately, the world. Our platform provides a space for thinkers, innovators, and change-makers to share their insights and inspire our community.

Inspiring Innovation

Through powerful ideas and meaningful conversations

What is TEDx?

In the spirit of ideas worth spreading, TEDx is a program of local, self-organized events that bring people together to share a TED-like experience.

At a TEDx event, TED Talks videos and live speakers combine to spark deep discussion and connection in a small group.

These local, self-organized events are branded TEDx, where x = independently organized TED event.

The TED Conference provides general guidance for the TEDx program, but individual TEDx events are self-organized.

About NMIMS Indore

NMIMS (Narsee Monjee Institute of Management Studies) Indore is a leading educational institution committed to academic excellence and holistic development. Our campus provides the perfect environment for intellectual discourse and innovative thinking.

As part of the prestigious NMIMS university network, our Indore campus embodies the values of excellence, innovation, and social responsibility that make it an ideal host for TEDx events.
